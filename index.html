0. 良率改善工程全景圖 (Process Overview) 0. Yield Enhancement Process Overview ▼

現代半導體製程（先進邏輯 3nm/2nm、3D NAND、DRAM、BCD 車用高壓晶片）牽涉 500 至 1,500 道繁複步驟。良率工程師（YEE）的使命是在極短的產品生命週期內，將良率從研發試產階段（NPI: 30%–50%）快速爬坡至成熟量產高地（HVM: >95%），並壓低車用市場要求的零缺陷（Zero Defect / < 1 DPPM）。 Modern semiconductor manufacturing involves between 500 and 1,500 individual process steps. The mission of Yield Enhancement Engineers (YEE) is to rapidly accelerate wafer yield from NPI ramp (30%–50%) to mature high-volume production (>95%) while achieving sub-DPPM reliability required by automotive and mission-critical applications.

💡 良率工程師的核心座右銘 Core Tenet of Yield Enhancement

「良率不是測試測出來的，是製程做出來的；但如果沒有高解析度的測試追溯與統計分解，你永遠不知道製程在哪裡犯了錯。」——本平台提供全套統計模型、機台共模算法與即時互動工具，協助工程團隊以實證數據驅動決策。 "Yield is not inspected or tested into a product; it is manufactured into it. However, without high-resolution test traceability and statistical decomposition, you will never know where the process failed." — This platform empowers engineering teams with empirical models, chamber commonality algorithms, and real-time interactive analyzers.

Phase 0: 良率基線與損失解構 (Baseline & Loss Decomposition) Phase 0: Baseline Setting & Yield Loss Decomposition ▼

進行任何良率改善前，第一步必須對「良率（Yield）」進行嚴謹的層級解構。混淆「線良率」與「晶粒良率」，或將「功能性失效」與「參數型飄移」混為一談，是良率專案最致命的方向性錯誤。 Before initiating yield enhancement, the first mandatory step is rigorous loss decomposition. Confusing Line Yield with Die Yield, or lumping Functional Fails with Parametric Drift, will misdirect the entire engineering campaign.

0.1 線良率 (Line Yield) 與 晶粒良率 (Die Yield) 0.1 Line Yield (FAB Out/In) vs. Die Yield (Sort/CP)

整體晶圓廠最終產出良率由兩大乘數構成： Overall fab shipment yield is the product of two distinct components:

\[ Y_{overall} = Y_{line} \times Y_{die} = \left(\frac{Wafers_{completed}}{Wafers_{started}}\right) \times \left(\frac{Dies_{pass}}{Dies_{tested}}\right) \]
  • Line Yield (\(Y_{line}\))： 晶圓製造過程中的完整性比例。損失主因為晶圓破片（Wafer Breakage）、機台刮傷（Robot Scratches）、化學槽沾污報廢（Scrap）、製程參數重大出界整批作廢。成熟晶圓廠目標通常 \(\ge 98.5\%\)。 Wafer integrity during processing. Losses stem from breakage, robotic handler scratches, bath contamination, and catastrophic excursions. Target is typically \(\ge 98.5\%\).
  • Die Yield (\(Y_{die}\))： 在完工晶圓上，電性與功能測試通過的良好晶粒比例。也是良率工程（Yield Enhancement）的核心主戰場。 The fraction of functioning chips on completed wafers passing wafer sort (CP). This is the primary battleground of Yield Enhancement Engineers.

0.2 系統性良率損失 vs. 隨機性良率損失 0.2 Systematic vs. Random Yield Loss

任何測試結果的良率損失均應分離為系統性缺陷（Systematic）與隨機微粒缺陷（Random）： Total die yield must be mathematically decoupled into systematic and random mechanisms:

\[ Y_{die} = Y_{sys} \times Y_{rand} = Y_{sys} \times f(A, D_0) \]

⚠️ 致命盲點：把「系統性問題」當作「微粒問題」處理 Fatal Trap: Treating Systematic Flaws as Random Particles

若晶圓良率損失主要是由微影光學鄰近效應（OPC）導致的 Via 假接觸，花費數百萬更換無塵室空氣濾網（HEPA）或加強機台洗機，良率將完全「紋風不動」。必須透過空間晶圓圖辨識與失效分析（FA）精確切出真因。 If yield loss is caused by sub-optimal Optical Proximity Correction (OPC) on marginal vias, spending millions on cleanroom HEPA filters will produce zero yield improvement. Rigorous spatial pattern classification and FA are mandatory to isolate the true mechanism.

1.2 座標系與 Notch / Flat 對齊陷阱 1.2 Coordinate System Alignment & Notch Orientation Trap

在結合 Inline Klarf 缺陷與 CP Wafer Map 時，工程團隊經常踩坑：測試機台的座標原點與晶圓物理凹口（Notch）方向不一致。 When correlating Inline Klarf defects with CP wafer maps, teams often fall into the trap of coordinate and notch orientation mismatch between inspection and tester tools.

⚠️ 座標對齊黃金法則 (Golden Rules for Coordinate Normalization) Golden Rules for Coordinate Normalization
  • Notch 歸一化：所有機台圖形統一旋轉至 Notch 向上（0°）或向下（180°）為單一基準。
  • 原點定義：確認 (0,0) 是晶圓幾何物理中心、左下角晶粒，還是光罩曝光陣列的 Reference Die。
  • 晶片步進尺寸 (Die Pitch) 與劃面道 (Scribe Line) 寬度驗證：浮點數誤差累積會導致周邊 Die 錯位判定。

Phase 3: 機台 Chamber 共模統計歸因 (Chamber Commonality Attribution) Phase 3: Equipment Chamber Commonality & Multivariate Attribution ▼

一座大型 12 吋晶圓廠中，同一製程步驟常配置 5–20 台同型設備，每台設備具備 2–6 個平行反應腔體（Chambers）。當良率突降時，如何從成千上萬個排列組合中，精準指認真兇？ In a 300mm mega-fab, a single process step may deploy 5–20 identical tools, each housing 2–6 reaction chambers. When a yield excursion hits, how do we systematically isolate the culprit chamber among thousands of combinations?

3.1 共模分析檢定統計法 3.1 Statistical Tests for Commonality

  • 卡方獨立性檢定 (\(\chi^2\) Test of Independence)： 檢定「晶片良率通過與否」與「是否經過特定機台腔體」兩變數是否相互獨立。若 \(p < 0.001\)，強烈拒絕獨立虛無假說。 Tests whether pass/fail outcome is statistically independent of chamber routing. A \(p < 0.001\) strongly rejects the null hypothesis.
  • 勝算比 (Odds Ratio, \(OR\))： 量化經過該機台之晶圓相較其他機台的不良風險倍數。\(OR > 2.0\) 且具統計顯著性時列入一級嫌疑機台。 Quantifies risk escalation for wafers routed through a specific chamber. An \(OR > 2.0\) with statistical significance indicates prime suspect status.
  • 單因子 / 雙因子變異數分析 (One-Way / Two-Way ANOVA)： 應用於連續型參數（如 WAT 飽和電流、薄膜膜厚），檢定不同腔體間的群體平均值是否存在顯著差異。 Applied to continuous metrology parameters (WAT current, film thickness) to verify inter-chamber mean divergence.

3.2 致命陷阱：辛普森悖論 (Simpson's Paradox) 與混淆變數 3.2 The Simpson's Paradox & Confounding Traps

🚨 辛普森悖論實戰案例警示 Simpson's Paradox Real-World Alert

現象：表面統計顯示 Chamber #1 的整體良率比 Chamber #2 低 5%。工程團隊判定 Chamber #1 異常。
真相：Chamber #1 專門被生管（PC）排程跑高難度的大晶粒旗艦產品（Die Area 400mm²），而 Chamber #2 全都在跑簡單成熟的小晶粒產品（40mm²）。分組產品分層後，Chamber #1 在同一產品上的良率其實反而更高！
鐵律：共模分析前，必須對產品型號（Product Device）、批次配方（Recipe）、晶圓槽位（Slot ID）進行分層校正（Stratification），否則將造成災難性誤判。 Phenomenon: Aggregate data shows Chamber #1 yield is 5% lower than Chamber #2. The team flags Chamber #1 as defective.
Reality: Production control routed complex large-die products (400mm²) exclusively to Chamber #1, while Chamber #2 processed smaller legacy products (40mm²). When stratified by product, Chamber #1 actually exhibited superior performance!
Golden Rule: Always stratify data by Device, Recipe, and FOUP Slot before drawing chamber commonality conclusions.

Phase 4: 參數 Margin 與 Cpk 飄移 (Parametric Margin & Cpk Drift) Phase 4: Parametric WAT/CP Margin & Cpk Drift ▼

功能性失效（Functional Open/Short）固然致命，但現代半導體良率競爭的勝負手在於「參數型良率損失（Parametric Yield Loss）」。晶片電路各節點皆可動作，但因臨界電壓（\(V_{th}\)）、驅動電流（\(I_{on}\)）、寄生電阻過大，導致工作頻率或功耗無法達標。 While functional opens/shorts cause clear-cut fallout, the competitive battlefield lies in Parametric Yield Loss. Circuits function logically, but marginal threshold voltages (\(V_{th}\)), drive currents (\(I_{on}\)), or parasitic resistances degrade performance below datasheet limits.

4.2 動態 Part Average Testing (PAT) 篩選技術 4.2 Dynamic Part Average Testing (Dynamic PAT)

傳統規格界限（LSL / USL）是靜態的。在車用晶片中，若某顆晶粒雖然在規格內（例如 Pass），但其量測數值顯著背離同批晶圓分佈中位數超過 \(4\sigma\)（稱為 Maverick Die），該晶粒內部必定隱藏亞微米級潛在結晶缺陷，進入車輛運作 6 個月後極高機率發生早期擊穿燒毀。 Traditional spec limits (LSL/USL) are static. For automotive chips, dies that pass static limits but deviate by more than \(4\sigma\) from the wafer median (Maverick Dies) harbor latent crystal anomalies that trigger field failures within operating life.

🚗 動態 PAT 計算公式 Dynamic PAT Formulation
\[ PAT_{LSL} = \text{Median} - 4 \times R_{IQR} / 1.349, \quad PAT_{USL} = \text{Median} + 4 \times R_{IQR} / 1.349 \]

以穩健四分位距（IQR）取代標準差，避免極端異常值干擾整體基準，達到零缺陷（Zero Defect）出貨防禦。 Uses robust Interquartile Range (IQR) to estimate dispersion immune to outliers, ensuring zero-defect shipment.

Phase 5: 失效分析 (FA) 實體物理驗證 (Failure Analysis & Physical Feedback) Phase 5: Failure Analysis (FA) & Physical Defect Correlation ▼

統計分析給出的是「嫌疑犯清單」，真正的「定罪證據」必須來自實體失效分析實驗室（FA Lab）。如果沒有將電性 Fail Bin 與物理缺陷特徵閉環比對，製程改善往往淪為盲人摸象。 Statistical correlations provide a suspect list; physical proof must originate from the Failure Analysis (FA) lab. Without closing the loop between electrical failure bins and physical defects, engineering actions remain guesswork.

分析階段FA Stage 儀器技術Analytical Instrument 物理檢驗原理Operating Principle 檢測缺陷類型Defect Target
1. 非破壞故障定位 OBIRCH / TIVA 紅外線雷射激發局部電阻熱變化，偵測漏電流微小擾動。 金屬層短路、閘極氧化層微漏電（Gate Oxide Breakdown）。

Phase 7: Ramp 學習曲線與客戶稽核治理 (Yield Ramp & Audit Defense) Phase 7: Yield Ramp Modeling & Customer Audit Defense ▼

新晶圓廠或新製程世代的投產，良率並非瞬間躍升，而是遵循指數型學習曲線（Yield Learning Curve）。在面對車用一級大廠（Tier 1）或國際客戶嚴苛稽核時，工程團隊必須提出合乎科學的良率爬坡模型與變更管理規範（4M1E）。 During new node introductions, yields do not surge instantaneously; they obey an empirical Yield Learning Curve. When undergoing rigorous Tier-1 automotive customer audits, engineering teams must present sound mathematical ramp projections and strict 4M1E change control.

\[ Y(t) = Y_\infty - (Y_\infty - Y_0) e^{-k \cdot C(t)} \]

其中 \(Y_0\) 為初始試產良率，\(Y_\infty\) 為極限成熟良率，\(C(t)\) 為累計生產晶圓數量（Cumulative Wafer Volume），\(k\) 為工程團隊的良率學習速率常數（Learning Rate）。 Where \(Y_0\) represents initial pilot yield, \(Y_\infty\) is asymptotic ceiling yield, \(C(t)\) is cumulative wafer volume, and \(k\) is the organizational learning rate constant.

📋 車用客戶稽核 4M1E 變更管制核對清單 IATF 16949 / 4M1E Change Management Checklist
  • Man (人員)：機台操作人員與製程工程師訓練認證證照。
  • Machine (設備機台)：新機台 Chamber 認證報告、Tool-to-Tool Matching 偏差量測。
  • Material (原材料)：化學研磨液、光阻批號變更前之 5 批 Pilot 試產良率對照。
  • Method (製程方法)：配方溫壓調整是否有完整 ECN/PCN 審批記錄與風險評估。
  • Environment (環境監控)：無塵室微氣候溫度（\(\pm 0.5^\circ\text{C}\)）、濕度（\(\pm 2\%\)）、AMC 氣體微污染監測。
